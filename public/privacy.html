Privacy policy

Vi's Scrub Deck does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • Your study progress, streak, quiz scores, and any cards you write are saved only on your own device.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.

For study only

Vi's Scrub Deck is a study aid. It is not medical advice and not a clinical reference. Reference ranges differ between labs, textbooks, and facilities. Always follow your course materials, your instructor, and your facility's policy.
